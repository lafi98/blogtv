{{siteName}} is a home for clear, practical crochet help. We write for people who are picking up a hook for the first time, coming back to crochet after a break, or ready to try their next project — and we try to explain not just *what* to do, but *why*, so you can fix problems on your own.

## What you'll find here

- **Beginner lessons** that start from a slip knot and build up one skill at a time.
- **Stitch guides** with US terms and their UK equivalents, plus chart symbols.
- **Free patterns** with stitch counts for every row or round, approximate sizes and yarn amounts, and notes on how to adjust them.
- **Tips and troubleshooting** for the problems everyone runs into: uneven edges, wavy circles, tension, reading patterns and yarn labels.

## Editorial standards

- **Accuracy first.** We use standard US crochet terminology, give UK terms alongside, and list hook sizes in millimetres with US sizes. Where crocheters genuinely do things differently, we say so instead of pretending there is only one right way.
- **Patterns you can follow.** Stitch counts are included and checked for every row or round. Sizes and yardage are labelled as estimates, because they depend on your yarn and tension.
- **Original content.** Everything we publish is written for this site. Traditional designs such as the classic granny square are written up in our own words.
- **Honest about AI.** We use AI tools to help draft some articles. Every article goes through automated checks for accuracy, originality, readability and policy issues before it can be published, and AI-assisted articles are labelled at the end of the article.
- **Independent.** We don't publish sponsored posts or paid reviews, and advertisers have no say in what we write.
- **Corrections.** If you spot a mistake, please [tell us](/contact). We fix errors promptly and update the article.
