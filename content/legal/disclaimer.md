Sizes and yarn amounts in patterns are estimates — always check your gauge and buy a little extra yarn.

## Errors and corrections

We check our articles carefully, but mistakes can slip through. If you find an error in a stitch count, a measurement or anything else, please [let us know](/contact) and we'll correct it.

## Safety

- Keep scissors, needles and small parts away from young children, and supervise children while they craft.
- For items made for babies and toddlers, avoid buttons, beads and plastic safety eyes that could be choking hazards — embroider details instead — and check the safety rules that apply to toys where you live.
- Crochet is not a substitute for professional medical or therapeutic advice.
